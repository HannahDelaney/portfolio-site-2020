intro-p: <a href="https://tepapa.govt.nz">Te Papa Tongarewa Museum of New Zealand</a> is a world-class, bicultural institution at the heart of Wellington's waterfront. In Commercial Marketing, my role supported the many teams at work to keep the museum, as well as their partner organisation, Tākina Wellington Convention and Exhibition Centre, operational. These teams included Te Papa Foundation, Te Papa Store, Te Papa Tours, Tākina Events and Te Papa Venues, Te Papa Cafés, Visitor Services, Commercial Events, Touring Exhibitions, and Partnerships. This page is a showcase of some of my projects during my tenure there.

img-1-client: Te Papa Store
img-1-location: External wall of museum

img-1-p: To promote Toa Tamariki (kid's store), I designed this mural that sits near both the main entrance of the museum, and onsite daycare. The design shows the updated storefront branding and is meant to appeal in particular to passing children by showcasing there is a slide, as well as all the beautiful items available for purchase. It was a huge honour to create an artwork that has become a part of the museum, with my illustrations and own handwriting built into the English and te reo Māori info text.

This informational video was originally created to be featured at the Wellington iSite Visitor's Centre on Level 1 of Tākina for international tourists, but was quickly also adapted to play on external and internal screens at Te Papa, to encourage all visitors to book one of the many tours available.

video-5-p: Being at the physical centre of New Zealand's capital city means that both Te Papa Venues and Tākina Events host various high profile events each year, with award winning chefs on hand for a quality visitor experience. With multiple annual menus and two buildings with distinct branding, I was tasked with creating templates that can quickly identify which menu, and which building an event is happening in. In addition to what's shown here, I created all the general venue and special event menus, as well as dietary versions and seat place cards, for each building.
