About Me.

Hey there! I’m Hannah, and I’m a maker.

I love drinking tea, being barefoot and bad jokes. I sing loudly, often, and have spent far too much of my memory real estate on movie quotes.

Motivated by continuous learning, a strong moral compass, and a sense of play, I love gaining a new skillset and get the most fulfillment out of projects that focus on justice, sustainability, or social enterprises that help disadvantaged folks. Discovering that art and design is what I can offer to serve others has allowed me to fully commit to my passion for it.

It would be lovely to get to know you and discover how we can create the solution you need for a brighter day tomorrow!
